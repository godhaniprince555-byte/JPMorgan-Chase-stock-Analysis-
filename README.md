# JPMorgan Chase Financial Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive **financial and investment analysis of JPMorgan Chase & Co. (JPM)**. The analysis combines fundamental financial analysis, valuation techniques, risk assessment, and technical forecasting to evaluate the company's financial health, stock performance, and investment potential.

The project was completed as part of the **DAB401 – Financial Analysis of Business** course at St. Clair College.

The analysis uses historical financial statements and stock market data to evaluate JPMorgan Chase through financial ratios, CAPM, WACC valuation, Free Cash Flow analysis, Monte Carlo Simulation, Facebook Prophet forecasting, and moving-average trading signals.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze JPMorgan Chase's business operations and competitive environment.
* Evaluate financial performance using key financial ratios.
* Calculate Beta and expected return using the **CAPM model**.
* Estimate the company's **Cost of Equity and WACC**.
* Calculate Free Cash Flow and assess financial sustainability.
* Estimate the intrinsic value of JPMorgan Chase stock.
* Forecast short-term stock prices using **Monte Carlo Simulation** and **Facebook Prophet**.
* Analyze trading signals using **20-day and 50-day Simple Moving Averages (SMA)**.
* Develop an overall investment recommendation based on fundamental and technical analysis.

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Financial Ratio Analysis

Key financial ratios were calculated to evaluate profitability, liquidity, leverage, and market valuation.

| Financial Metric         | Result |
| ------------------------ | -----: |
| Operating Profit Margin  | 39.90% |
| Net Profit Margin        | 31.20% |
| Return on Assets (ROA)   |  1.29% |
| Return on Equity (ROE)   | 15.70% |
| Earnings Per Share (EPS) | $20.02 |
| Current Ratio            |   0.49 |
| P/E Ratio                |  14.17 |
| Debt-to-Equity Ratio     |   1.40 |
| Dividend Payout Ratio    | 27.70% |
| Sustainable Growth Rate  | 11.35% |

These metrics were used to assess JPMorgan Chase's profitability, financial stability, shareholder returns, and growth potential.

---

### 2. CAPM & Beta Analysis

The **Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)** was used to estimate the required return on equity based on systematic market risk.

The regression analysis produced:

* **Beta:** 1.09
* **R²:** 0.8653
* **Correlation:** 0.93
* **Risk-Free Rate:** 4.38%
* **Market Return:** 13.50%
* **Expected Return:** 14.32%

A Beta above 1 indicates that JPMorgan's stock is slightly more sensitive to market movements than the overall market.

---

### 3. Free Cash Flow Analysis

Free Cash Flow was calculated as:

**FCF = Operating Cash Flow − Capital Expenditures**

The project calculated:

**Free Cash Flow = $30.2 billion**

The strong FCF indicates the company's ability to generate cash internally and support dividends, share buybacks, debt reduction, technology investment, and business expansion.

---

### 4. WACC & Valuation

The Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) was calculated using the company's cost of equity and after-tax cost of debt.

Key results:

* **Cost of Debt:** 4.73%
* **After-Tax Cost of Debt:** 3.74%
* **Market Value of Equity:** $800.23 billion
* **Equity Weight:** 61.2%
* **Debt Weight:** 38.8%
* **WACC:** 11.0%
* **Estimated Intrinsic Value:** $338.60 per share
* **Market Price Used:** $283.77
* **Estimated Upside:** Approximately 19.3%

Based on the valuation model, JPMorgan Chase appeared undervalued relative to the market price used in the analysis.

## 📈 Technical Analysis

### Monte Carlo Simulation

A Monte Carlo simulation was used to estimate possible JPM stock prices over a 30-day period.

| Metric               |  Result |
| -------------------- | ------: |
| Current Market Price | $283.77 |
| Mean 30-Day Forecast | $291.45 |
| Forecast Low         | $232.31 |
| Forecast High        | $351.13 |

The simulation indicated a slight upward bias while also highlighting potential market volatility and downside risk.

### Facebook Prophet Forecast

The Prophet forecasting model showed:

* A consistent upward trend.
* Continued expected price growth.
* Relatively narrow confidence intervals.
* A generally positive short-term outlook.

### SMA Trading Strategy

A technical trading strategy based on **20-day and 50-day Simple Moving Averages** was also evaluated.

The analysis examined:

* Buy signals
* Sell signals
* Bullish momentum
* Market corrections
* Sideways-market noise

The strategy was particularly useful for identifying major upward trends, although sideways markets generated more frequent signals.

## 💡 Key Findings

* JPMorgan Chase demonstrates strong profitability with a **31.2% net profit margin**.
* ROE of **15.7%** indicates effective use of shareholder equity.
* The company generated approximately **$30.2 billion in FCF**.
* The calculated sustainable growth rate was **11.35%**.
* The WACC valuation estimated an intrinsic value of **$338.60 per share**.
* The estimated intrinsic value was approximately **19.3% higher** than the market price used in the analysis.
* Monte Carlo simulation indicated both upside potential and downside risk.
* Technical forecasting models showed an overall positive stock-price trend.
